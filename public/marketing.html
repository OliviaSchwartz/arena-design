Is Arena for you?

You're a fit if you:

  • Lead brand, integrated, growth, audience, or experiential marketing at an established company
  • Want relationships across sports, media & entertainment, not just within your own discipline
  • Live and work in the US or Canada
  • Want to meet people outside your usual circle

Probably not a fit if you:

  • Are mainly job hunting (Arena isn't a job board)
  • Want to sell services to members
  • Are a student or early in your career
