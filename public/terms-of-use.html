30-Day Guarantee Terms & Conditions

Eligibility

To qualify for a full refund under Arena’s 30-Day Guarantee, members must meet all of the following conditions during their first 30 days of membership:

1. Profile Completion

Complete your full Arena member profile by May 15, 2026 at 11:59 PM ET. This includes all required fields, preferences, and any information needed to generate quality matches.

2. Weekly Intro Review

Curated intros are delivered every Monday at 10:00 AM ET. Members must review and respond to all intros within 24 hours of delivery each week.

3. Scheduling Meetings with Matches

When a mutual match is confirmed, members must schedule a meeting within 48 hours. If a scheduling conflict arises, members are expected to proactively communicate and propose an alternative time within 48 hours.

4. Attending Scheduled Meetings

Members must attend all meetings they have scheduled with matches — no no-shows. If you need to reschedule, communicate proactively and rebook through Arena or the scheduling tool associated with your match.

5. Responding to Match Communications

Members must respond to messages from matches within 48 hours.

6. Event Participation

Members must attend at least one (1) live member event or watch the orientation session (live or replay) during the 30-day guarantee period. Three events are held during each cohort cycle.

How it works

If you’ve met all of the above conditions and don’t find value in your Arena membership after 30 days, email support@arenatalent.com within 7 days of your guarantee period ending to request a refund. Refund requests will be reviewed and verified against participation activity.

What This Guarantee Is

This guarantee is designed for members who fully engage with the process and genuinely give it a shot. Members who do not meet the participation requirements above are not eligible for a refund under this guarantee.

A Note on Matches

We recognize that connection is a two-way street. If a match is unresponsive or cancels on their end, that will not count against your eligibility.
