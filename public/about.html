OUR ORIGIN

Built by industry insiders.

We spent 25+ years combined at companies including Disney, NFL, LinkedIn, and NBA — and from the inside, we noticed the same patterns play out.

The thoughtful DMs that never got a reply. The flood of irrelevant AI-generated cold ones. Expensive conferences that turn into mentally draining marathons. Coffee chats that end in a handshake and nothing after.

None of it relevant and all of it random. The best relationships left to chance or stuck inside the circle you already had.

OUR BELIEF

Sports, media, and entertainment run on relationships.

Leagues operate like media brands. Studios move like strategic partners. Platforms shape culture, commerce, and careers simultaneously. And yet — who you know still depends largely on where you went, who responded, and whether the timing was right.

It's not a networking problem. It's an infrastructure problem.
OUR METHOD

Arena makes the right introductions happen — intentionally.

No feed to scroll. No Slack chat to monitor. No directory to dig through. Three curated introductions, every Monday — matched to your experience, your goals, and where you operate across sports, media, and entertainment.

For every intro, you see their background, their goals, and how they're open to collaborating. You decide which conversations you take.
