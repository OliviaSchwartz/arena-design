MEMBER TYPES

Four paths into Arena.

We accept members across these categories. Each has its own requirements.

Industry Leaders

Manager-level or above, currently working in-house for an established sports, media, or entertainment organization. Founders with prior industry experience and an established company with brand recognition in the space are also welcome.

Leagues & teams Networks & studios Publishers Venues & live events Record labels Agencies (50+ employees)
Brand Leaders

Leaders from established brands that actively partner or activate within sports, media, or entertainment.

Sponsorship Activations Brand marketing
Athletes

Current or former professional athletes actively building, investing, or creating within sports, media & entertainment.

Brand building Investing Content creation
Consultants, freelancers, startups & agencies

We accept a limited number with established industry careers and/or active work with leading enterprises. We also limit acceptance for people who own or work for smaller talent agencies (under 50 employees).

To keep the community a solicitor-free space, consultants, freelancers, and agency professionals agree not to sell services to members — instead, build relationships and let collaboration happen naturally.
