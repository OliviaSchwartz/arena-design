Is Arena for you?

You're a fit if you:

  • Are manager through C-suite, in-house at an established sports, media or entertainment company

  • Live and work in the US or Canada
  • Want to meet people outside your usual circle

Probably not a fit if you:

  • Are mainly job hunting (Arena isn't a job board)
