Who Arena Academy is for

Arena Academy is for ambitious early-career professionals in sports, media & entertainment who are actively building in the industry. The program brings together proactive students and rising stars who know the peers rising alongside them today will be their most valuable relationships tomorrow.

We're looking for applicants who meet all of the following criteria:

01

Are early in their careers.

College juniors and seniors, graduate students early in their careers, graduates from the past two years, and early-career individual contributors up to senior coordinator level (titles like coordinator, associate, or analyst). Current and recently graduated student athletes are encouraged to apply.

02

Are active in the sports, media & entertainment industry.

Internships, jobs, passion projects, leadership roles, volunteer work, and content you've created all count, as long as it's 1) related to the business of sports, media & entertainment, and 2) current or from the past two years.

03

Take initiative.

You go after opportunities, take on projects and leadership roles, and share what you're building and learning in sports, media & entertainment.

04

Value peer relationships.

You invest in the people coming up alongside you and see those connections as key to your long-term growth.

05

Have an up-to-date LinkedIn profile.

It should reflect what you're working on today.

06

Are based in the US or Canada.

At this time, we are not able to accept members who are based outside of the US or Canada. Study abroad programs are still eligible.

Come ready to connect. Make the most of your peer intros and mentor sessions, and join live when you can.

Relationships and skills, not job placement. This isn't a job board or a placement service. We help you build the relationships and skills to take your next step.

FAQs

Do I need experience in the industry?

You don't need a paid role, but you do need current or recent involvement in the business of sports, media & entertainment, such as internships, projects, leadership roles, or content you've created. Note: Your LinkedIn profile is a key part of our review, so make sure everything is up to date and reflects what you’re working on now.

How are applications reviewed?

Every application is reviewed by our admissions committee against the Arena Academy criteria, and not everyone who applies is accepted. We look for early-career professionals who are proactively learning, building, and sharing what they’re doing in the sports, media & entertainment industry, take initiative, and value building relationships with their peers.

I worked in the industry a while ago and want to get back in. Is Arena Academy a good fit?

Arena Academy is designed for people who are early in their careers right now, and the program is built around that shared stage. If you're returning to the industry after time away, you can stay connected as other programs take shape by subscribing to our newsletter at arenatalent.com/newsletter.

What if I miss a session?

Joining live is the best way to get your questions answered, but it's not required. Every session is recorded and the slides are shared, so you can catch up on your own time.

How do the peer intros work?

Starting in Week 2, you'll get three curated intros each week to others in your cohort, matched on your goals and interests. Accept or pass on each one. When you both accept, it's a match, and you can book time to meet virtually directly on each other's calendars.

Who are the mentors?

Leaders from the Arena network who work in the business of sports, media & entertainment at brands, leagues, teams, studios, agencies, platforms, venues, and beyond. After admissions, you'll be placed in a small group of aligned peers with a mentor matched to your career goals. You'll have one group session and one individual session together.

Is this a job placement program?

No. Arena Academy is about building the relationships, skills, and industry knowledge that help you take your next step.
