Applications for Arena’s Summer Cohort Set to Open on July 20

Arena is opening applications for its Summer 2026 Cohort on July 20, with the window open through August 14.

NEW YORK, NY July 14, 2026 — Arena, the curated member network redefining how sports, media & entertainment executives connect and collaborate, is opening applications for its Summer 2026 Cohort on July 20.

Arena brings together executives across brands, leagues, teams, studios, networks, publishers, venues, and record labels through curated weekly introductions designed to spark meaningful professional relationships. Since launching, Arena has facilitated more than 10,000 curated introductions among executives and industry leaders across sports, media and entertainment.

Arena welcomes established applicants from across the sports, media, and entertainment industries, from managers and C-suite executives to creators and professional athletes, all interested in building the connections that will shape the industry’s future in marketing, partnerships, content, community, performance, events, and beyond.

Every Arena member is vetted by the company’s membership review committee to ensure alignment with the community’s goals and curated network. Members also gain access to in-person networking events held throughout the year at major industry moments, such as Super Bowl, SXSW, and WNBA All-Star Weekend.

Arena opens limited application windows throughout the year. Arena’s Summer Cohort applications can be found at arenatalent.com, available from July 20th to August 14th.

About Arena

Arena is a curated member network for leaders across sports, media, and entertainment. Through weekly curated introductions and intimate industry gatherings, Arena helps members build industry relationships that lead to new partnerships, investments, and career opportunities. Led by industry insiders from the NFL, Disney, NBA, and LinkedIn, Arena is building the network where the industry meets.
